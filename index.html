OnTrack · Workout tracker for Android

Log fast. Plan ahead. See your progress.

OnTrack is a workout log built for the gym floor: big buttons, fast set entry, and a history that shows how far you've come. No account, no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

OnTrack

Built for the minute between sets

Everything you need mid-workout is one tap away. Everything else stays out of the way until you want it.

  • Log fast

    Tap to complete a set. New sets fill in from the last time you did that exercise, so you rarely type. Track weight and reps, time or distance, with warm-up sets, RPE and notes.

  • Plan ahead

    Build templates like Push Day or Leg Day. Starting one sets up every set at your planned weight and reps. Put templates on a weekly routine or on specific dates.

  • Circuits and timers

    Timed rounds with work and rest cues that keep going with the screen off, plus a stopwatch, countdown and plate calculator.

  • See your progress

    Personal records are detected automatically. Lifetime stats and charts, body measurements and progress photos show how far you've come.

  • Heart rate and Health Connect

    Pair a chest strap or a watch that broadcasts heart rate to record it live. Save workouts and measurements to Health Connect, and bring in readings from your other apps.

  • Foldables and tablets

    On a big screen OnTrack shows more, not just bigger: overviews beside your lists, a weekly dashboard, and your timer and heart rate beside your sets.

Your data stays yours

OnTrack has no account and no servers. We never see your workouts.
