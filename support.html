Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. OnTrack works without an account or sign-in. Your workouts are stored on your phone, and we never receive them.

Is OnTrack free?

Yes. Logging workouts, up to 10 templates, history, personal records, backups and Health Connect are free, with no ads. OnTrack Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds more tools and insights. There's no subscription.

What does OnTrack Pro add?

Unlimited templates, advanced stats and charts, the plate calculator and interval timer, progress photos and tape measurements, heart-rate zones, custom circuits, and RPE, drop sets, sets to failure and form-check videos.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone?

Pro belongs to the Google account you bought it with. Sign in to Google Play with that account, then open Settings → OnTrack Pro → Restore purchase.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On Android, turn on Back up to Google Drive in OnTrack's Settings, then install OnTrack on your new phone and restore from that backup. The same backup can move your data to OnTrack for iPhone. Android's own device backup also restores OnTrack when you set up a new Android phone, except for progress photos.

Can I export my workouts?

Yes. OnTrack exports your history to a CSV file you can open in a spreadsheet, and it can import CSV files from other workout apps.

Which heart-rate monitors work?

On Android, any Bluetooth heart-rate monitor that broadcasts heart rate: a chest strap, a Garmin watch with Broadcast Heart Rate turned on, or a Pixel Watch with heart-rate sharing turned on.

Does OnTrack work with Health Connect or Apple Health?

Yes, if you turn it on. On Android, OnTrack can save workouts, heart rate, weight and body fat to Health Connect, and bring in weight and body fat from your other apps. On iPhone, it works with Apple Health.

How do I delete my data?

Delete any workout, measurement or photo in the app, or uninstall OnTrack to erase everything on that phone. If you turned on Google Drive backup, also delete the OnTrack folder in your Drive. We never receive your data, so nothing is stored on our side.
